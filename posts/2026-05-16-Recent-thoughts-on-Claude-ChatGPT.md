I've been rigorously using ChatGPT or Claude or Gemini Pro or Copilot; some version of this "AI chat interfaces + coding assistants" for over 6 months now. Although it has helped in many dead end scenarios; help me understand the situations and navigate through it, it also has been messing up with my programming skill.

Am not a expert programmer, but i can do stuff, build things. And lately I've been totally relying on these tools to just do everything. I love typing as well, but I used to like a heavy chunk of that typing going into coding, and right now, it's just type the fucking prompts/contexts - whatever. Just keep explaining things. I used the voice modality for input as well, but try to use typing many times so i don't loose that as well.

Now, my company is providing all these tools, Codex - Claude - Copilot. And they are generous subscriptions, where i can do a lot with rarely hitting limits. But that is the problem, am clearly hitting any of these AI interfaces, when starting anything new. Nothing is built from scratch, it's all rosy picture of starting from a decent platform that is set by these fuckers.

What this makes me realise is, this way of AI first of approaching any task that is new to you, devoids the opportunity of learning nitty gritty of it. Learning on own brings situations where you explore multiple alternatives and in process learn the tradeoffs of which option to chose and when.

So as of now, it's just all perfects steps (big ones) towards completion of the tasks. And am sure, am going through AI psychosis. No idea how I will improve this situation, given that the management people will expect everything to be done superfast, be of great quality, and so on..
